How it works

From first measurement to a decision in weeks, not months

Capture

Measurement kits go in at the points that matter: compressor discharge, shaft columns, level take-offs, pump stations. No shutdowns, no cut pipes.

Analyse

Data from every instrument lands on one timeline in FlowVerification Analytics. Flow is lined up against shifts, blasting windows and production.

Act

Each finding comes with a cost attached and an owner. Re-measure after the fix and the dashboard shows whether the saving actually landed.

The analytics platform

Built around how a mine actually runs

Generic dashboards show you a line. FlowVerification Analytics shows you the line against your shift cycle, your targets and your tariff.

Every utility on one clock

Air, water, pressure, temperature and power are time-aligned, so you see cause and effect, not five separate spreadsheets.

Shift-aware baselines

Set targets for drilling, blasting and off-shift periods. Anything drawing outside its window is flagged as waste.

Cost in Rands

Flow is converted to energy and money using your own tariffs, so findings are ranked by what they're worth.

Productivity per team

Compare air, water and power use per drilling team or section, and see which crews get the most out of each cubic metre.

Equipment efficiency

Track compressor specific power, pump efficiency and aftercooler performance over time, not just on audit day.

Reports people read

One-page summaries for management, full data for engineers. Before-and-after views prove the saving.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to shut anything down to install it?

No. Liquid flow is measured with clamp-on ultrasonic sensors on the outside of the pipe. Compressed air and gas probes go in under pressure through a standard 1" ball valve. Power is measured with flexible Rogowski coils and voltage clamps. Systems keep running throughout.

Does it work underground without network coverage?

Yes. Capture instruments log on their own and data is collected when they're retrieved or come back into range. Analytics then lines everything up on one timeline.

Can it use data from meters we already have?

In most cases, yes. Logger exports and existing site meter data can be brought into Analytics alongside FlowVerification Capture data. Tell us what you have and we'll confirm.

Is this a one-off audit or ongoing monitoring?

Either. Many sites start with a short deployment to find the biggest savings, then keep monitoring the areas that matter to prove the fixes stick.

Who owns the data?

You do. Dashboards are shared only with people you nominate, and you can export the full dataset whenever you want it.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of measuring points and how long you monitor. Book a demo and we'll scope a trial deployment for your site.
